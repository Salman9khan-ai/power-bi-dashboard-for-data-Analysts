# power-bi-dashboard-for-data-Analysts
### *Key Sections & Findings*  

1. *Average Salary by Job Title*  
   - A bar chart showcases salaries across different roles, with *Data Scientists* earning the highest average salary, followed by Data Engineers and Data Architects.
   - Other job titles include Data Analyst, Database Developer, and those who are currently students or looking for work.

2. *Countries of Voters*  
   - A treemap visually represents the distribution of survey takers by country.
   - The largest segments belong to professionals from *the United States and India*, with smaller contributions from the United Kingdom, Canada, Nigeria, and other regions.

3. *Survey Participation & Demographics*  
   - The dashboard highlights the *total number of survey participants* as *630*.
   - The *average age* of participants is *29.87 years*, providing insights into the age demographics of data professionals.

4. *Programming Language Preferences*  
   - A bar chart reveals the most favored programming languages among survey participants.
   - *Python* leads the pack as the most preferred language, followed by R, C/C++, JavaScript, and Java.

5. *Challenges in Handling Data*  
   - A donut chart illustrates the perceived difficulty of working with data.
   - The majority (42.7%) find it *easy*, while others categorize it as difficult, very difficult, very easy, or neutral.

6. *Job Satisfaction Metrics*  
   - *Happiness with Work-Life Balance: Displayed using a gauge, with an average score of **5.74 out of 10*.
   - *Happiness with Salary: Another gauge indicates an average rating of **4.27 out of 10*, reflecting salary satisfaction levels.

### *Impact & Use Cases*  
This dashboard is a valuable tool for analyzing trends in the data profession. It can help:
- *Aspiring data professionals* understand job prospects and salary expectations.
- *Employers and recruiters* gauge job satisfaction levels and preferred skill sets.
- *Industry analysts* identify trends in programming language usage and demographic distributions
